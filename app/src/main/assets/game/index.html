VOIDRUNNER

DON'T DODGE THE LIGHTNING. STEAL IT.

Every arcade shooter ever made teaches you the same lesson: when the storm comes, run. VOIDRUNNER teaches the opposite. The lightning is not the threat — it's the ammunition. Fly INTO the storm, let the arcs overcharge your ship, then turn around and spend that stolen power on everything shooting at you. Cowards dodge. Runners steal.

THIS APP — THE FRONT DOOR

This app is VOIDRUNNER's front door on your Seeker: the game's front page — what it is, how it plays, what it costs — plus a native wallet connection. The full game arrives in this app as an update; it is not included in this build. What's here today is the door, the story, and your key.

THE GAUNTLET (in the full game)

100 waves of bullet-hell survival. A named boss every 5 waves. Ten weapons, each with its own mastery track. A seeded endless mode when the campaign falls.

ARCADE PRICING, HONESTLY

A free day pass gets you in the door, then 25¢ a play. $3.50 buys 14 plays; $7 buys 30 days unlimited.

In THIS release, every price is in simulated play units — play money. This build takes no payments and charges no real money, anywhere.

YOUR WALLET, YOUR KEYS

Connect your Seed Vault wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter and approve signatures on-device — optional, and your keys never leave your wallet. This app never touches them, holds no funds, and requires no sign-in.
