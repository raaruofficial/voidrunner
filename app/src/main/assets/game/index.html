VOIDRUNNER

Don't dodge the lightning. Steal it.

A bullet-hell survival game for the Solana Seeker. In VOIDRUNNER the lightning buffs you: fly into the storm, harvest the charge, and turn it back on the fleet. One hundred waves, a named boss every five, and a seeded endless mode when the campaign falls.

THE GAME

  • 100 waves — ten boss families, telegraphed charges, escalating swarms
  • Signature weapons — Starlance, Quasar, Halcyon and more, each with its own slot and mastery crown
  • Excalibur sorties — your swords wake themselves when a boss pushes you to the edge
  • .skr sign-in — one identity across the arcade, via Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0
  • Arcade pricing — 25¢ a play, $3.50 for 14 plays, $7 for a 30-day pass
  • Support in SKR — a voluntary donation ladder for pilots who want to feed the funnel

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag to move — your ship fires on its own
  • Fly through lightning to charge; charged shots hit harder
  • Swap weapons mid-run to match the swarm
  • Survive the boss at every fifth wave

This shell build carries VOIDRUNNER's front page. The full live game — profiles, economy, leaderboards — runs on the VOIDRUNNER service and connects through this app's native wallet bridge (window.VoidrunnerWallet).
