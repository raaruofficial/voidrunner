What it is

Every arcade shooter ever made teaches you the same lesson: when the storm comes, run. VOIDRUNNER teaches the opposite. The lightning is not the threat — it's the ammunition. Fly INTO the storm, let the arcs overcharge your ship, then turn around and spend that stolen power on everything shooting at you. Cowards dodge. Runners steal.

Built for Seeker

VOIDRUNNER is built by Raaru, exclusively for the Solana Seeker.

The Gauntlet

100 waves of bullet-hell survival, tuned to stay intense but fair. A named boss every 5 waves: learn THE CAPACITOR's fan traps, outlast the AEGIS TYRANT — and at wave 100, ION ABSOLUTE cycles everything you've survived into one final exam. Ten weapons, ten personalities — the ARC MELTER, the STARLANCE, the RAILSPIKE — and every weapon carries its own mastery track that levels forever on your profile. Profiles and leaderboards track every run.

Arcade pricing, honestly

A real cabinet, real rules:

  • Day pass (first sign-in) — Free
  • Single play — 25¢
  • 14 plays — $3.50
  • 30 days unlimited — $7

No subscriptions sneaking up on you, no cash-value credits, nothing to win but the run itself.

In THIS release, every price is in simulated play units — play money, labeled as such. This build takes no payments and charges no real money, anywhere.
